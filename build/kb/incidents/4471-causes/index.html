The three causes

parser (#1, fixed). The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. Day and month swap. It covers app and email reports everywhere, and booking-form reports at tenants on booking-v2, whose hint already says DD/MM/YYYY.

hint (#2, won’t fix: booking-v3 is retired). The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as told, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. It only shows up in booking-form reports from tenants on booking-v3. A parser fix does not touch it.

one-day (#3, open). A few bookings move by one day: Tuesday shows as Monday, an invite arrives before the booking, one client gets Tuesday and another in Toronto gets Monday. A swap cannot do that; time zones can. The desk already listed these reports (oneDayMove in desk-notes.json). Its view counts them under their tenant’s cause, so the table does too, and gives them a row of their own.

How to tell them apart

  • Go by the tenant and the way the report came in, never by intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, so an en-US browser at a day-first tenant gets hint either way.
  • A date that moved by one day belongs to #3, whatever cause its tenant files it under.
  • The export repeats reports across pages: count each id once.
  • The subject line tells you nothing here. Every subject the hint and one-day reports use also appears in parser reports (“Month and day swapped?” among them), so decision 0001 merges all three into one ticket.
